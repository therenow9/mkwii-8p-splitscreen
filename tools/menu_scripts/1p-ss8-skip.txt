# The 8 Players page's skips at 1P (Transmission Vanilla, Drift Keep Last): Grand Prix, Mario, the first kart; the
# transmission page is skipped and the drift page shows (P1's first pick since the title), then the cup page. B there
# passes the drift page back to the kart page; A passes it again to the cup page. GC port 1.
setting SETTING_SS8TRANSMISSION SS8TRANSMISSION_VANILLA
setting SETTING_SS8DRIFT SS8DRIFT_KEEPLAST
wait 900
press 1 a        # title
wait 175
press 1 a        # license
wait 175
press 1 a        # main menu: Single Player
wait 240
press 1 a        # Grand Prix
wait 240
press 1 a        # 50cc
wait 300
press 1 a        # Mario
wait 300
press 1 a        # kart select
wait 300
press 1 a        # the drift page: P1's first pick since the title
wait 480         # the cup page, held for its shot
press 1 b        # back: the drift page passes back to kart select
wait 480         # kart select, held for its shot
press 1 a        # the drift page passes again
wait 600         # the cup page
