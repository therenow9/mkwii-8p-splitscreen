# Select Timer at 15 s, 1P Grand Prix: P1 moves off the opening choice on every page and never confirms, so with no
# pick yet each zero takes what P1 hovers; from the cup page Back to the character page, P1 moves off again, and each
# zero now takes P1's last pick. GC port 1.
setting SETTING_SS8SELECTTIMER SS8SELECTTIMER_15
wait 900
press 1 a        # title
wait 175
press 1 a        # license
wait 240
press 1 a        # Single Player
wait 240
press 1 a        # Grand Prix
wait 240
press 1 a        # 50cc
wait 400
press 1 down     # character page: off the opening choice
wait 1100        # the zero: the hovered one
press 1 down     # vehicle page
wait 1100
press 1 down     # transmission page
wait 1100
press 1 down     # drift page
wait 1100        # the cup page
press 1 b        # back to the drift page
wait 300
press 1 b        # transmission
wait 300
press 1 b        # vehicles
wait 300
press 1 b        # characters
wait 400
press 1 right    # off the last pick
wait 60
press 1 right
wait 1100        # the zero: the last pick
press 1 up       # vehicle page
wait 1100
press 1 up       # transmission page
wait 1100
press 1 up       # drift page
wait 1100        # the cup page
